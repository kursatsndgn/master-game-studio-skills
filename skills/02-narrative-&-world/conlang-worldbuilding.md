# Sub-Agent Domain: @game-writer / @narrative-director

## 1. Constructed Language (Conlang) Engineering

To avoid the "fantasy alphabet soup" trap, all fictional terms, factions, and place names must derive from systematic phonological rules.

### Step 1: Phoneme Inventory & Syllable Structure
- **Vowels:** `/a/, /e/, /i/, /o/, /u/`
- **Consonants:** Stops (`/k/, /t/, /p/`), Fricatives (`/v/, /s/, /x/`), Nasals (`/m/, /n/`), Liquids (`/r/, /l/`)
- **Allowed Syllables:** `(C)V(C)` (e.g., *Khor*, *Vel*, *Tak*, *Sil*)
- **Forbidden Clusters:** No triple consonants (e.g., no *'strk'* or *'ngth'* clusters)

### Step 2: Root Lexicon Table
| Root | Semantic Core | Derivative Words | English Equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Khor** | Solid, rock, endurance | *Khor-dun* (fortress), *Khor-ash* (miner) | Fortress of stone |
| **Vel** | Fluid, luminescent, flow | *Vel-in* (river), *Vel-mor* (liquid fuel) | River of light |
| **Zul** | Void, hollow, absence | *Zul-tir* (exile), *Zul-ka* (empty shell) | The outcast |

## 2. Belief Systems & Theological Matrix

Factions and religions must be grounded in physical material realities rather than generic good vs. evil tropes.

| Belief System | Material Basis | Theological Axiom | Taboo / Blasphemy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Forge-Pact** | Scarcity of uncorrupted iron | "The fire tests what words conceal." | Quenching steel with stagnant water. |
| **The Tide-Order** | Dependence on geothermal springs | "Flow is life; stagnation is decay." | Damming a natural thermal vent. |
| **The Obsidian Watch** | Shards used for electrical batteries | "Charge must return to the earth." | Hoarding dead cells inside living quarters. |

## 3. Settlement & Spatial Topography Guidelines
When designing towns, outposts, and levels:
1. **Resource Triad:** Every settlement must visibly possess (1) potable water/energy, (2) defensive barrier, (3) primary economic export.
2. **Architectural Trace:** High status buildings occupy high elevations or clean bedrock; industrial waste drains toward lower districts.
3. **Environmental Signage:** All in-world warning signs must use conlang pictographs with consistent symbology.
